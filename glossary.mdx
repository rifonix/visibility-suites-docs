title: "Glossary"
description: "Every product term in Visibility Suite, defined in plain English."
---

## A

**Action plan** The prioritized list of tasks Visibility Suite generates from your monitoring results. Also called Opportunities. See [the action plan](/optimize/opportunities).

**Agent readiness score** A 0 to 100 grade for how readable your website is to AI crawlers, with letter grades from A+ to F. See [website readiness](/optimize/agent-readiness).

**AI platform** An AI assistant or AI search surface you monitor: ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot, Grok, Google AI Overview, or Google AI Mode.

## B

**Battleground** A single question where you and your competitors compete for AI recommendations. Battlegrounds are labeled won, contested, or lost. See [competitive analysis](/insights/competitive-analysis).

**Brand hub** Your business profile inside a project: names and aliases AI might call you, competitors, and audience details. See [brands and competitors](/setup/brands-and-competitors).

## C

**Citation** When an AI answer lists your website as a source. Tracked as citation rate and pages cited. See [citations](/insights/citations).

**Cross-location report** An organization-level view that rolls up scores across all your location projects. See [multi-location tracking](/setup/multi-location).

## F

**Fan-out query** A follow-up search an AI platform runs behind the scenes while answering a question. Visibility Suite captures these so you can see how AI researches your market.

## M

**Mention** When an AI answer names your business. Tracked as mention rate.

**Monitoring schedule** How often your questions are asked on each platform: weekly by default, daily on higher plans. See [schedules](/setup/schedules).

## O

**Opportunity** One suggested task in your action plan, with a priority score and an effort label like quick win.

**Opportunity score** The priority number attached to each suggested task, so you know what to do first.

## P

**Pages cited** The specific pages on your website that AI platforms used as sources.

**Prompt** One question you monitor, phrased the way a customer would ask it.

**Prompt battleground** See **battleground**.

**Project** One business or location under monitoring, with its own questions, competitors, and reports.

## R

**Recommendation rate** The share of AI answers where your business is recommended as a top pick, not just mentioned.

**Run** One question asked once on one AI platform. Plans include a monthly run allowance; extra runs are available as run packs.

**Run pack** A one-time top-up of extra runs that does not change your plan. See [plans and billing](/account/plans-and-billing).

## S

**Same brand, other location** How Visibility Suite labels a mention of a different branch of your own chain, so it is not confused with a competitor.

**Sentiment score** How positively or negatively AI talks about your business, from 0 to 100, where 50 is neutral. See [sentiment](/insights/sentiment).

**SERP Radar** The module that tracks classic Google results (local map pack, people-also-ask, related searches) next to your AI visibility. See [SERP Radar](/insights/serp-radar).

**Share of ad voice** Your share of the advertising that appears inside AI answers for your questions. See [ads monitoring](/insights/ads-monitoring).

**Share of voice** Your share of brand appearances across AI answers, compared with your competitors.

**Subfolder tracking** Monitoring a location that lives on a subfolder of a larger site, like `example.com/stores/denver`.

## T

**Topic** A product or service category you want to be known for, used to group questions and reports.

## V

**Visibility score** The share of tracked AI answers that mention or cite your business, from 0 to 100.
