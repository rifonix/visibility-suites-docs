When AI answers a question, it often lists its sources. Those sources are citations, and they tell you two things: whether AI trusts your website enough to quote it, and which websites it trusts instead. A citation matters even without a mention, because being the source today is how you become the recommendation tomorrow.

## The KPI cards

- **Total Citations**: every source link captured across your tracked answers.
- **Unique Domains**: how many distinct websites those links point to.
- **Pages**: distinct cited pages.
- **Own Brand**: how much of that citation activity points at your domain.

## The domains leaderboard

"Domains by Citation Count" ranks the websites AI cites most on your questions, with each domain's citation count, share of all citations, and type. Filter between "External" sources and your own brand, and toggle between "Latest run" and "Comparing" to see movement across runs.

Expand any row to see the cited pages on that domain and how many times each was cited. This is your answer to "why does AI recommend them?": often the competitor is not preferred, their specific page is.

## Your own pages

The "Own-brand Citations" view lists your pages AI has used as sources. Every cited URL is classified by AI into a page type (blog article, service page, menu, FAQ, locations page, and dozens more), so you can see patterns like "most of my citations come from one blog post" or "my service pages never get cited".

Those patterns are actionable:

- **One page does all the work**: build more pages in the same format for your other topics.
- **A page type never appears**: AI does not treat that format as a good source in your market. Check what winning domains use instead.
- **No own-brand citations at all**: start with your action plan in Opportunities, and check Agent Readiness, since AI crawlers may be unable to read your site.

## Why AI trusts these sources

Read the leaderboard as a trust map. The domains at the top are where AI goes to verify claims about your market: review platforms, local directories, news coverage, and whichever business websites answer questions most directly. If a competitor outranks you here, open their cited pages and compare depth, structure, and freshness against yours.

## Following a citation back to the answer

Every citation links back to the prompt and the exact AI answer it came from. When a domain surprises you, open the answer and read how AI used that source: quoted as fact, listed among options, or linked for details. Prompt detail pages also show citation domains per question, so you can see which sources win for which topics.
