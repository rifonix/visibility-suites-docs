The Overview is the first screen to check each week. It answers one question: when customers ask AI about your market, how often does your business come up, and is that getting better or worse?

## The KPI cards

Four numbers sit on top:

- **Visibility Score** (0 to 100): the share of AI answers where your brand appears, mentioned or cited. If half your tracked answers include you, the score is 50.
- **Brand Sentiment** (0 to 100): how positively AI talks about you. 50 is neutral, above is positive, below is negative.
- **Brand Mentions**: how many times AI answers named your business. This is sentence-level: an answer naming you three times counts as three.
- **Pages Cited**: how many distinct pages on your website AI used as sources.

Each card shows a delta comparing your most recent monitoring run against the first run in the selected date range.

## The charts

- **Platform Visibility**: your score broken down per AI engine, switchable between columns and a line view. A platform where you score near zero is a platform where customers never hear about you.
- **Share of Voice**: a donut of mentions across you and your tracked competitors. Your slice versus the rest is the market position AI currently gives you.
- **Top Cited Domains**: the ten websites AI relies on most in your market. Expect directories and review sites; the question is whether your own domain appears.
- **Topic Performance**: visibility, mentions, and sentiment per topic. Use it to find the services you are known for and the ones AI ignores.
- **Sentiment Trend**: the daily tone of your mentions over the selected period.

## Filtering and comparing

Two filters sit in the header:

- **Date range**: last 7, 30, or 90 days, or a custom range. All deltas recompute against the first run in the range.
- **Platform**: narrow everything to a single AI engine, or keep "All platforms".

While a monitoring run is in progress, a banner lets you know results are streaming in and the dashboard fills as they arrive.

## Exporting a PDF report

The export button downloads the Overview as a PDF, named with your date range (for example `overview-2026-09-01_to_2026-09-30.pdf`). Use it for owner updates, franchise reports, or agency check-ins.

<Tip>
  A single run is a snapshot; AI answers vary. Watch the direction over several runs before changing strategy.
</Tip>
