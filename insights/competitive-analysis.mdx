Competitive Analysis compares your brand against the competitors you track in Brand Hub, question by question. If the page is empty, add competitors in Brand Hub and come back after the next monitoring run.

## The KPI strip

- **Citation share**: your slice of all citations across tracked brands.
- **Citation win rate**: how often you are cited on questions where competitors appear too.
- **Closest rival**: the competitor breathing down your neck, or the one you are chasing.
- **Losing battlegrounds**: questions where competitors were cited and you were not.

## The rival scorecard

One table, every tracked brand: visibility, sentiment, citation share, mention and citation presence, share change, and each brand's top cited page type. Use the focus selector to compare against the whole compset or zero in on a single rival. The scorecard answers "who is winning overall"; the battlegrounds below answer "where, specifically".

## Prompt battlegrounds

Each tracked question gets a win state:

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Won** | You are cited or recommended; competitors are not |
| **Contested** | Both you and competitors appear |
| **Lost** | A competitor is cited; you are absent |
| **Mention-only risk** | You are named, but a competitor is cited as the source |
| **No signal** | Neither side appears meaningfully |

Filter the battlegrounds table by topic or win state, and use the opportunity score (0 to 100) to find the questions worth attacking first. For each battleground you see the best competitor, the competitor pages being cited, and your own citation evidence. Three actions sit on every row: open the prompt detail, review opportunities, or plan content.

Mention-only risk deserves special attention: AI knows your name but trusts a competitor's website as the source. That usually means their content answers the question better than yours.

## Format battlegrounds

This diagnostic shows citation volume by brand and page type, revealing structural gaps: maybe rivals win with comparison pages while you rely on your homepage, or their FAQ pages get cited while you have none.

## New competitors the AI finds

The analysis reads every answer for brand-like names you do not track yet. These discovered competitors wait in Brand Hub for triage: promote a real rival to tracked status, dismiss aggregators and directories, or merge a duplicate into a brand you already track. Checking this list after runs is how you learn about new entrants before they show up in your numbers.
