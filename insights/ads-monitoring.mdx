Ads are appearing inside AI answers and AI search results. The Ads page tracks who is buying that visibility on the exact questions you monitor, so you can see when competitors pay to show up where you are trying to earn recommendations.

## The KPI cards

- **Ad Presence**: the share of captured results that contained ads.
- **Share of Ad Voice**: your share of all advertising seen on your questions, versus every other advertiser.
- **Prompts with Ads**: how many of your tracked questions trigger advertising.
- **Unique domains**: how many distinct advertisers appeared.

An "Ad Volume Over Time" chart shows whether advertising in your market is growing. Each ad is labeled by source: Google or AI Ads.

## The tables

- **Advertisers**: every domain seen advertising, marked "Own Brand" or "Competitor", with show rate and how often it appeared in range. Select an advertiser to see what they ran.
- **Prompts That Trigger Ads**: the questions where ads appear. A question that attracts ads is a question with proven commercial intent. If you are invisible there organically, competitors are buying the customer instead.
- **Sponsored Ads** and **Own-brand Ads**: creative samples, so you can read the actual copy competitors run.

## New advertiser alerts

After each monitoring run, Visibility Suite notifies you when a competitor domain advertises on your questions for the first time, with a pointer to the Ads page for share of ad voice and creatives. It is an early-warning system: the moment a rival starts paying for your market, you know.

## Sharing the AI Ads Exposure Report

You can create a public link to a read-only "AI Ads Exposure Report" for your project. Anyone with the link can view it; no account needed. Viewers see:

- The share of AI answers that contained ads
- The ad volume trend over time
- Top advertisers by share of ad voice, with the option to tap an advertiser and see their ads
- Your own brand separated from competitors

The report carries a "Get your free AI visibility report" footer, which makes it useful as a leave-behind for prospects, franchisees, or stakeholders. Links can be revoked, and a revoked link shows as invalid.

<Note>
  Share links expose a read-only view of your ads data to anyone who has the link. Create one per audience and revoke it when it has served its purpose.
</Note>
