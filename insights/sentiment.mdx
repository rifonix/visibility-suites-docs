Visibility tells you whether AI talks about you. Sentiment tells you what it says. The score runs from 0 (very negative) to 100 (very positive), with 50 as neutral.

## The main view

Four cards break down the tone of your mentions:

- **Sentiment Mix**: a donut of positive, neutral, and negative mentions across the selected period.
- **Sentiment Trend**: the daily breakdown, so you can spot the day the tone shifted.
- **Brand Sentiment**: scores per tracked brand, yours and your competitors'.
- **Platform Sentiment**: scores per AI engine. It is common for one platform to praise you while another shrugs.

<Note>
  Some mentions cannot be judged by the classifier (for example a bare name in a list). These are flagged and excluded from averages rather than guessed.
</Note>

## Drilling into a brand or platform

Select any brand or platform for its detail view: total mentions, score, distribution, trend, and a breakdown by the other dimension. Below the numbers is the part that matters:

- **Top Positive** and **Top Negative**: the highest-impact sentences AI wrote about that brand.
- **Recent Mentions**: the latest sentences, with the platform and question behind each.
- **Citations & Sources**: the pages AI was reading when it formed its opinion.

Every score is grounded in sentence-level evidence. When AI says "service can be slow at peak times", you see that exact sentence, the platform that said it, and the question that triggered it.

## Acting on negative drivers

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read the negative quotes">
    Look for repeating themes: price, wait times, a specific service, outdated information. One bad sentence is noise; a pattern is a signal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the sources">
    AI forms opinions from what it reads. Open the citations behind negative mentions: often the culprit is a stale review page or an old directory listing, not your own site.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fix the source, then the story">
    Update your pages, encourage fresh reviews where the problem is visible, and use Opportunities or Content Studio to publish content that answers the concern directly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch the trend">
    Sentiment lags reality. Confirm the fix is landing by watching the Sentiment Trend over your next runs.
  </Step>
</Steps>
