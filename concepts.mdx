A few concepts unlock everything else in the product. This page explains them in plain English, in the order you will meet them.

## Projects

A project tracks one business in one market. Everything (questions, competitors, scores, reports) lives inside a project. If you run two locations, you create one project per location. If your website uses subfolders for locations, like `example.com/locations/austin`, a single project can track a subfolder directly.

## Topics

Topics are the things you want to be known for: "family dining", "emergency plumbing", "teeth whitening". They come from your website during setup, and they group your questions and reports so you can see which parts of the business are visible and which are invisible to AI.

## Prompts

A prompt is one question you monitor, written the way a real customer asks it: "what's the best rooftop restaurant in Nashville?" The setup wizard generates prompts from your topics and location, and you can add your own anytime.

Every prompt is asked on a schedule, and the full AI answer is saved each time. You can open any answer and read exactly what a customer would have seen.

## Runs

A run is one prompt asked once on one AI platform. If you track 20 questions on 5 platforms, one weekly cycle is 100 runs. Runs are the unit your plan measures; your monthly plan includes a run allowance, and you can top up anytime with run packs.

## AI platforms

The seven AI surfaces you can monitor: ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot, Grok, Google AI Overview, and Google AI Mode. Google search (traditional results) is tracked alongside them through SERP Radar.

## Mentions, citations, and recommendations

Three ways your business can appear in an AI answer, from weakest to strongest:

| Appearance | What it means | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Mention** | Your business is named in the answer | "Other options include Luigi's on Main." |
| **Citation** | AI used your website as a source | Your menu page appears in the source list |
| **Recommendation** | AI suggests you as a top pick | "For authentic wood-fired pizza, go to Luigi's." |

A citation is valuable even without a mention: it means AI trusts your site as a source, which is how you become the recommendation later.

## Visibility score

Your visibility score is the share of AI answers where your brand appears, either mentioned or cited, from 0 to 100. If half of your tracked answers include you, your score is 50. It is tracked over time and can be broken down by platform, topic, and prompt.

## Share of voice

Share of voice compares your visibility to your competitors' across the same questions. If AI mentions you in 30% of answers and your closest rival in 60%, you know exactly how much ground there is to close.

## Sentiment

When AI talks about you, what does it say? Sentiment scores the tone of your mentions from 0 (very negative) to 100 (very positive), with 50 as neutral, and shows you the exact sentences it scored.

## Agent readiness

Agent readiness is a 0 to 100 scan of your website that checks whether AI crawlers can find and read your content: your robots rules, sitemap, structured data, and more. A site AI cannot read is a site AI cannot recommend.
