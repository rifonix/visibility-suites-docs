## Contact us

Email [hello@visibilitysuite.ai](mailto:hello@visibilitysuite.ai) for anything: bugs, billing questions, data requests, feature ideas, or a second pair of eyes on your results. We reply by email.

One address covers it all, so you never have to figure out which department to contact. Say what you need in your own words and it reaches the right person.

## What to include in a report

The more specific your message, the faster the fix. When something looks wrong, include:

- **Your project name**, so we look at the right business
- **The prompt**, if the issue is about one question
- **The platform**, if it concerns a specific AI like ChatGPT or Perplexity
- **A screenshot** of what you see, especially for errors or odd numbers
- **What you expected** versus what happened

For publishing issues, add the error shown in Publishing history. For scan issues, add your website address.
