This guide takes you from a new account to monitoring how AI talks about your business. The setup takes about 5 minutes, and your first results arrive with your first scheduled run.

## Prerequisites

- A business with a website
- An email address to create your account
- Roughly 5 minutes

You do not need any technical skills or access to your website's code.

## Set up your project

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to [app.visibilitysuite.ai/sign-up](https://app.visibilitysuite.ai/sign-up) and create a free account. No credit card is required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a new project">
    A project tracks one business or one location. Enter your business name and website address. Visibility Suite reads your website to understand what you offer and where you operate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm what you offer">
    The setup wizard lists the products and services it found on your site. Edit the list so it matches what you want to be known for. These become the topics you track.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your questions">
    Based on your topics and location, the wizard generates the questions real customers ask AI, like "best brunch in Scottsdale" or "emergency plumber near me". Add, remove, or edit them. Each question is called a prompt.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your AI platforms">
    Choose which AI assistants to monitor: ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot, Grok, Google AI Overview, and Google AI Mode. You can change this later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your competitors">
    The wizard suggests competitors it finds in your market. Keep the ones that matter to you. AI answers are benchmarked against these businesses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch monitoring">
    Review everything and start your schedule. Monitoring runs weekly by default. Each run asks every question on every platform you picked and saves the full answers.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Your first results

After your first run completes, open your project to see:

- **Visibility score**: how often AI mentions or cites you, from 0 to 100
- **The exact answers**: every AI response, saved and searchable
- **Who gets recommended instead**: the competitors AI picks, per question
- **Your action plan**: prioritized suggestions to improve

<Tip>
  AI answers vary from day to day. Give monitoring two to three weekly runs before drawing conclusions, and watch trends rather than any single answer.
</Tip>
