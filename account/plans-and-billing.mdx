Visibility Suite is metered in runs. Your plan sets how many runs and seats your organization gets each month, and you can top up anytime.

## Plans

| Plan | Monthly | Annual | Included runs per month | Seats |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Free** | $0 | $0 | 0 | 1 |
| **Launch** | $29 | $290 | 2,000 | 3 |
| **Grow** | $49 | $490 | 12,000 | 10 |
| **Scale** | $99 | $990 | 40,000 | 25 |

The Free plan lets you set up a project and explore the app. Monitoring, and the optimization features below, require a paid plan.

Runs are shared across all projects in your workspace: one pool, every project draws from it. Settings shows your current usage and a forecast based on your active projects, so you can see the month coming before it bills.

## What a run is

One run is one prompt asked once on one AI platform. If you track 20 questions on 5 platforms, one weekly cycle costs 100 runs. Adjust your prompt count, platform count, or schedule to fit your allowance.

## Overage and top-ups

If you pass your included runs on a paid plan, extra runs bill at **$0.01 per run**.

To raise your monthly allowance instead of paying overage, add a run pack:

| Run pack | Runs per month | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Small | 1,000 | $29/mo |
| Medium | 5,000 | $119/mo |
| Large | 20,000 | $399/mo |

Need more people or more businesses instead?

- **Extra seat**: $15/mo per person
- **Extra project slot**: $19/mo per additional project

## Manage your subscription

Billing is handled through a secure billing portal, opened from inside the app.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Go to Settings and find Organization usage. Your plan status, included runs, forecast, and run packs live here.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change plan">
    Select Change plan to upgrade or downgrade. Changes apply to your whole organization.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage billing">
    Manage billing opens the billing portal, where you can update payment methods, download invoices, and cancel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cancel anytime">
    Cancel from the billing portal. Your plan stays active until the end of the paid period.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Invoices and receipts are available in the billing portal. For billing questions the portal cannot answer, email [hello@visibilitysuite.ai](mailto:hello@visibilitysuite.ai).
</Note>
