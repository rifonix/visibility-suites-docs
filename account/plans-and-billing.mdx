Visibility Suite plans are built around **tracked prompts** and **included projects**. Every paid plan includes the full AI engine set, unlimited team seats, and a monthly run allowance derived from your prompts and cadence. You can add capacity at any time.

## Plans

| Plan | Monthly | Annual | Effective monthly (annual) | Tracked prompts | Projects | Competitors per project | Cadence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Free Setup** | $0 | $0 | $0 | 0 | 1 | 3 | Preview only |
| **Starter** | $39 | $348 | $29 | 25 | 1 | 3 | Twice weekly |
| **Pro** | $149 | $1,404 | $117 | 75 | 3 | 10 | Twice weekly |
| **Business** | $399 | $3,828 | $319 | 200 | 10 | 25 | Daily or twice weekly |
| **Agency** | $1,199 | $11,988 | $999 | 600 | 100 | 25 | Daily |

Starter and Pro include a **7-day free trial**. The Free Setup plan lets you complete AI-guided onboarding, build your prompt and competitor plan, and preview the workspace before starting a trial. Monitoring requires a paid plan.

Every paid plan includes unlimited team seats. Usage is pooled across the organization, so all projects draw from the same prompt and run allowance.

## What each plan unlocks

**Starter** is for a single brand or local business: 25 tracked prompts, 1 project, 3 competitors, twice-weekly monitoring, visibility/sentiment/citation dashboards, 5 AI content drafts per month, and 1 readiness audit per month.

**Pro** adds the optimization suite for growing teams: 75 tracked prompts, 3 projects, 10 competitors per project, competitive analysis and battlegrounds, the Opportunities action plan, SERP Radar, AI ads tracking, Agent Chat, 30 AI content drafts per month, and 5 readiness audits per month.

**Business** is for multi-location brands and marketing teams: 200 tracked prompts, 10 projects, 25 competitors per project, daily or twice-weekly monitoring, the cross-location comparison matrix, MCP server and API keys, public share links, priority queue execution, 100 AI content drafts per month, and 15 readiness audits per month.

**Agency** is for agencies and enterprises: 600 tracked prompts, 100 projects, 25 competitors per project, daily monitoring, everything in Business, a fast-track queue, 300 AI content drafts per month, 50 readiness audits per month, and unlimited team and client seats.

## How runs are counted

One run is one prompt asked once on one AI platform. If you track 20 questions across 5 platforms, one monitoring cycle costs 100 runs. Included monthly runs scale with your plan:

| Plan | Included runs per month |
| --- | --- |
| Free Setup | 0 |
| Starter | 1,350 |
| Pro | 4,050 |
| Business | 36,000 |
| Agency | 108,000 |

If you pass your included runs on a paid plan, extra runs bill at **$0.01 per run**.

## Manage your subscription

Billing is handled through a secure billing portal, opened from inside the app.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Go to Settings and find Organization usage. Your plan status, included prompts and runs, forecast, and add-ons live here.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change plan">
    Select Change plan to upgrade or downgrade. Changes apply to your whole organization.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add capacity">
    Add a prompt pack, project slot, or expansion block from the same usage panel. Add-ons stack on top of your plan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage billing">
    Manage billing opens the billing portal, where you can update payment methods, download invoices, and cancel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cancel anytime">
    Cancel from the billing portal. Your plan stays active until the end of the paid period.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Invoices and receipts are available in the billing portal. For billing questions the portal cannot answer, email [hello@visibilitysuite.ai](mailto:hello@visibilitysuite.ai).
</Note>
