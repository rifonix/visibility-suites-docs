Your monitoring data is yours to take with you. Here is what you can export today and where everything comes from.

## PDF reports

The Overview page exports a polished PDF report of your visibility metrics for any date range: last 7, 30, or 90 days, or a custom range. Use the export button in the page header. The file downloads as `overview-{from}_to_{to}.pdf`, ready to email to a business partner, a boss, or a client.

<Note>
  Report exports are a paid plan feature. See [plans and billing](/account/plans-and-billing).
</Note>

## CSV exports

- **Projects list**: export your organization dashboard to CSV for a spreadsheet of all monitored businesses
- **Prompt library**: export your tracked questions to CSV from the Prompts page

## Share links

The Ads page can generate a public share link: a read-only AI ads exposure report showing who advertises on your tracked prompts. Anyone with the link can view it, no account needed, and you can revoke the link anytime. It is a useful way to show a client or partner what competitors are spending on.

## API access

Paid plans include API access to the same data the app uses: projects, prompts, runs, analytics, citations, and more. If you need scheduled exports or want to pull Visibility Suite data into your own reporting, start with the [API reference](/api-reference/introduction).

## Where your data comes from

Every number traces back to a real AI answer. Each monitoring run asks your tracked questions on your chosen platforms and stores the complete responses. Scores, trends, and reports are computed from those stored answers, and every metric in the app can be opened to reveal the exact AI response behind it. Nothing is sampled or estimated from third-party panels: it is your questions, asked on schedule, answered by the real platforms.

## Data deletion and retention

Questions about deleting your data, exporting everything you have, or how long records are kept? Email [hello@visibilitysuite.ai](mailto:hello@visibilitysuite.ai) with your organization name and what you need, and the team will handle it. See [support](/help/support) for what to include to speed things up.
