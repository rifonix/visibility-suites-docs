The bell icon at the top of the app is your notification center. A badge shows your unread count, and clicking the bell opens your recent alerts.

Notifications are in-app only. They live inside Visibility Suite, so you see them when you sign in, not in your email inbox.

## What alerts you get

**New advertiser alerts**

After a monitoring run, if a competitor domain is seen advertising on your tracked prompts for the first time, you get one alert naming the advertiser and how many ads and prompts were involved. It links you to the Ads page, where you can see their share of ad voice and the actual ad creatives. This is your early warning that a competitor has started paying to appear where your customers ask questions.

**Platform announcements**

Messages from the Visibility Suite team: new features, important changes, and service notices. These come from the platform, not from your monitoring data.

Alerts carry a severity (info, warning, success, or error), which controls how they are styled so urgent items stand out.

## Who sees what

Each notification has a scope:

- **Organization**: goes to everyone in your organization, like the new advertiser alerts
- **User**: addressed to you personally
- **Global**: platform-wide announcements for all accounts

Your bell shows the mix addressed to you and your organization. Your unread count is your own: marking something read does not clear it for teammates.

## Manage your notifications

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the bell">
    Click the bell to see recent alerts, newest first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open an alert to act on it">
    Alerts link to the relevant page, like the Ads report for a new advertiser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark as read">
    Opening an alert marks it read. Use mark all as read to clear a full list at once.
  </Step>
</Steps>
