<Update label="October 2026" description="Public launch" tags={["Launch"]}>
  Visibility Suite is publicly available. The launch includes:

  - **AI visibility monitoring** across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot, Grok, Google AI Overview, and Google AI Mode, on weekly or daily schedules
  - **Visibility score, share of voice, and recommendation rate**, with trend charts and full drill-down to the saved AI answers behind every number
  - **Citations intelligence**: which sources AI trusts in your market and which of your pages it cites
  - **Sentiment analysis** with sentence-level evidence
  - **Competitive analysis** with prompt battlegrounds and rival scorecards
  - **Action plan (Opportunities)**: prioritized, effort-labeled tasks generated from your visibility gaps
  - **Content Studio**: AI-drafted articles with publishing to your CMS through MCP connections
  - **Agent readiness scanner**: a 0 to 100 grade for how readable your website is to AI crawlers, with weekly re-scans
  - **SERP Radar**: Google results, map pack, people-also-ask, and related searches alongside AI visibility
  - **Ads monitoring**: detect ads inside AI answers and share a public AI ads exposure report
  - **Multi-location support**: subfolder tracking, per-location projects, and cross-location org reports
  - **REST API** covering monitoring, analytics, and project management
  - **Plans for local businesses**: free tier, Launch at $49/mo, Grow at $149/mo, Scale at $499/mo, with run packs for one-time top-ups
</Update>
