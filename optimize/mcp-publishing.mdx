title: "MCP publishing"
description: "Connect your CMS to Content Studio and publish approved drafts straight to your website, starting with WordPress."
---

MCP publishing sends finished Content Studio articles directly into your website's content system. WordPress is the first supported target.

MCP, the Model Context Protocol, is a standard way for two software systems to hand content to each other, so Visibility Suite can publish into your CMS the same way a person would, without you copying and pasting.

## What you need

- A paid plan with Content Studio access
- A website whose CMS exposes an MCP endpoint (for WordPress, your site URL with its MCP path, like `https://yoursite.com/mcp`)
- An API key or token if your endpoint requires one

If you are not sure whether your site has an MCP endpoint, ask whoever manages your website, or contact [support](/help/support).

## Connect your CMS

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the publishing section">
    In Content Studio, open Content publishing (MCP) and add a connection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the connection details">
    Give it a name, like "WordPress production", and the server URL your CMS provides.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose authentication">
    Pick the method your endpoint uses: none, a bearer token, or a custom header such as an API key header. Your secret is stored encrypted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the connection">
    Testing discovers the tools your CMS exposes: typically create content, update content, and upload media.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map the tools and fields">
    Tell Visibility Suite which tool creates articles, which updates them, and which handles images, and how fields like title, body, slug, and featured image map across.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a default destination status">
    Choose whether published pieces go live immediately or arrive as drafts in your CMS for a final look.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Publish a draft

<Steps>
  <Step title="Finish your article">
    Review the draft in Content Studio until you are happy with it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Choose the connection and publish. New pages are created, and republishing an already published piece updates it in place.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify on your site">
    Publishing history records every publish with its result and the external URL. Open published page takes you straight to the live article.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Safety notes

<Warning>
  Nothing publishes on its own. Every publish is an action you take on a draft you have reviewed.
</Warning>

- You approve each article before it leaves Content Studio.
- Every publish is logged in Publishing history, including failures, so you can see exactly what happened.
- Connection secrets are stored encrypted and never shown again after saving.
- To stop publishing, disconnect the connection. Already published pages stay on your site; the link between the systems is what closes.

<Tip>
  Set your default destination status to draft in your CMS for the first few publishes. It gives you one more review step inside WordPress before anything goes live.
</Tip>
