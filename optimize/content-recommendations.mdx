Content recommendations answer the question every business owner asks after seeing their scores: "so what should I actually publish?" Visibility Suite generates three to six concrete, publishable content pieces, each one scoped to win citations on the prompts where you are currently invisible.

<Note>
  Content recommendations are available on paid plans. See [plans and billing](/account/plans-and-billing).
</Note>

## Where the ideas come from

Every recommendation is built from your monitoring data, not guesswork:

- **Prompts you lose**: questions where competitors get cited and you do not
- **Never-cited prompts**: questions where your website has never appeared as a source
- **Topic gaps**: areas of your business with demand but no supporting content
- **Competitor content AI cites**: the exact pages winning citations for rivals, and the page types AI prefers
- **Google demand**: People Also Ask questions and related searches captured alongside your AI answers

## What each recommendation includes

- **Title**: action-ready, written for the customer question it targets
- **Format**: blog article, service page, product page, FAQ, comparison, guide, or case study
- **Target prompts**: one to four verbatim questions the piece should capture
- **Rationale**: which gap, competitor, or page-type pattern makes this worth writing
- **Section outline**: three to six suggested headings
- **Impact estimate**: what winning those prompts is worth

Recommendations keep a stable identity across regenerations, so your list stays recognizable as new data arrives.

## How ideas connect to the rest of the product

Content recommendations do not live in isolation. They are one end of a pipeline:

1. **Opportunities** tell you which ground to take. Content recommendations tell you what to publish to take it.
2. A recommendation (or an opportunity, or a SERP Radar insight) seeds a draft in **Content Studio** with the target prompts and context already attached.
3. Content Studio drafts, refines, and publishes the piece to your site.

You can also create content ideas directly from SERP Radar: any related-search demand can become a content angle with one click.

## Use a recommendation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Content recommendations">
    Find them alongside your opportunities in the Optimization area. Generate them manually, or wait for the automatic pass after a monitoring run.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick by impact">
    Compare the impact estimates and target prompts. A piece targeting four losing prompts usually beats one targeting a single prompt.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it to Content Studio">
    Start a draft from the recommendation. The title, outline, format, and target prompts carry over.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review, edit, publish">
    Refine the draft, then publish through your connected CMS. See [MCP publishing](/optimize/mcp-publishing).
  </Step>
</Steps>
