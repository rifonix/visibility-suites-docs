The Opportunities page is your action plan. After each monitoring run, Visibility Suite analyzes your business profile, your tracked questions, your citations, competitor citations, and Google search demand, then generates a short list of strategic moves to raise your visibility.

Instead of a generic checklist, each opportunity is tied to your data: the exact prompts you are losing, the competitors winning them, and the evidence behind the recommendation.

<Note>
  Opportunities are available on paid plans. See [plans and billing](/account/plans-and-billing).
</Note>

## What is in an opportunity

Each card on the board contains:

- **Title and description**: what to do and why, in plain language
- **Implementation steps**: the specific actions to take
- **Priority score**: 0 to 100, so you know what to attack first
- **Impact**: high, medium, or low
- **Effort**: Quick win, Medium, or Large, so you can spot fast progress
- **Category**: content, technical, branding, competitive, or local SEO
- **Target prompts**: the exact questions this move is meant to win
- **Success metric**: how you will know it worked
- **Evidence**: two to four observed facts from your own monitoring data
- **Competitor intelligence**: why a competitor currently wins this ground
- **Owner role**: whether this is a job for content, web, marketing, or operations

## When new opportunities generate

Opportunities generate automatically after each monitoring run completes. You can also generate them manually anytime from the Generate opportunities button. Generation analyzes your latest results, so a fresh run means fresher recommendations.

Your board state is safe across regenerations: each opportunity has a stable identity, so cards you already moved keep their place when new analysis arrives.

## Work the board

Opportunities live on a Kanban board with three columns: **To do**, **In progress**, and **Done**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Opportunities page">
    Find it under Optimization in the sidebar. If you see "No opportunities yet", run monitoring first or select Generate opportunities.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sort by priority">
    Start with the highest priority score. Filter for Quick win effort labels if you want progress this week.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move a card to In progress">
    Accepting a task is as simple as moving it to In progress. That tells your team (and yourself) what is actively being worked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Do the work">
    Content tasks have an Open in Content Studio button that starts a draft with the context already attached. Technical tasks list the steps for whoever manages your website.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move it to Done">
    Completing a task closes the loop. Your next monitoring runs will show whether the move worked, through the opportunity's success metric and your visibility trend.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Local SEO opportunities matter most for brick and mortar businesses: they target the map results, location pages, and "near me" questions that drive foot traffic.
</Tip>
