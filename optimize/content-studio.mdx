Content Studio is where content work happens. It drafts articles with AI, lets you edit everything, manages your images, and publishes to your website through your connected CMS. Every draft is tied to the project it serves: your brand profile, your competitors, and the exact questions you track.

## What you can create

Drafts come in the formats local businesses actually need: blog articles, service pages, product pages, FAQs, comparisons, guides, case studies, and social posts.

Each draft moves through three statuses:

- **Draft**: work in progress
- **Published**: pushed to your site through a publishing connection
- **Archived**: kept for reference, out of the active list

## Where drafts come from

You can start a New article from scratch, or let the product hand you a head start. Drafts can be seeded from:

- A **content recommendation**, with title, outline, and target prompts attached
- An **opportunity**, opened straight from the action plan
- A **SERP Radar insight**, created from Google search demand

The Suggested briefs list keeps these starting points in one place.

## Why the writing sounds like you

AI drafting in Content Studio is grounded in two things generic writers do not have:

1. **Your brand knowledge**: the profile from your Brand Hub, covering what you do, where you operate, your products and services, and your positioning. If the writing drifts off-brand, use Edit brand context to correct the source.
2. **Your monitoring evidence**: the questions you track and the answers AI gives today, so articles answer real customer questions instead of imagined ones.

Output is clean, structured HTML written for AI search engines: answer the question early, give definitive answers, and use headings that match how people ask.

## Draft and refine

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a starting point">
    Choose a suggested brief, a recommendation, or start a new article. Attach target prompts so the article answers the questions you are losing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate the draft">
    The AI expands your brief and outline into a full article. Review it like you would a writer's first draft.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refine with Improve and Optimize">
    Request an AI revision and you get a before and after diff. Accept change keeps it, Reject discards it. You always see exactly what would change before it changes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add media">
    Upload images to your media library for featured or inline use. Alt text is required: it is how AI engines understand your images, and it keeps your site accessible.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Send the finished piece to your website through an MCP connection, and track it in Publishing history. See [MCP publishing](/optimize/mcp-publishing).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Articles that target specific monitored prompts close visibility gaps fastest. Check the target prompts panel before publishing to confirm the piece answers them directly.
</Tip>
