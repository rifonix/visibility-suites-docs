Brand Hub is where your business identity lives inside a project. Everything monitoring depends on (what counts as a mention of you, who you are compared against, what context the AI uses) is managed here. Onboarding fills most of it in, and you can refine it anytime.

## Your brand profile

The profile holds your brand name, website, description, industry, location, language, and your products and services. It also includes fields the AI fills in and you can edit:

- **AI detected themes**: the subjects your website talks about, used to keep prompts and analysis on-brand.
- **Audience segments**: the customer groups AI detected from your site, like "families with young kids" or "corporate event planners".
- **Identity tags** and **context notes**: extra facts that help the analysis understand your positioning, nearby landmarks, and markets.

<Tip>
  Keep your products and services list current. It feeds topic suggestions, prompt generation, and the AI that builds your action plan.
</Tip>

## Mention aliases

Aliases are the alternate names AI assistants might use for your business: an old name, an abbreviation, a common misspelling, or a "the ... on Main Street" phrasing. When Visibility Suite scans an AI answer, it matches mentions against your brand name and every alias.

This matters because AI rarely uses your exact registered name. If your restaurant is "The Oak Room at Hillside" but ChatGPT calls it "Oak Room Hillside", an alias is the difference between a counted mention and an invisible one. Add every variant you have seen customers or AI use.

## Tracked brands and competitors

The tracked brands list is everyone you monitor: yourself ("Myself") and your competitors. Competitors come from two places:

- **Auto-discovered competitors**: brands the AI finds named in real answers, sorted by how often they are mentioned. Select "Track" to promote one into your tracked list.
- **Manual entries**: competitors you add yourself with a name and website.

Removing a brand deletes it and its aliases from the project. The AI keeps reading answers for new names, so check the discovered list regularly: competitors you have never heard of sometimes show up there first.

## Same brand, other location

If you run multiple locations, mentions of your other branches can pollute the data. Marking a brand as "same brand, other location" tells Visibility Suite: this is us, not a rival. Those mentions are separated from true competitors in rankings and leaderboards, so your share of voice reflects the market, not your own sister stores.

<Note>
  Competitive reports only include tracked competitors. If your Competitive Analysis page looks empty, add competitors here and wait for the next monitoring run.
</Note>
