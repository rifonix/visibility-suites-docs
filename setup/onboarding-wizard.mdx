title: "The onboarding wizard"
description: "A step-by-step tour of the 7-step setup wizard, what AI fills in at each step, and what happens when you launch."
---

The onboarding wizard turns your website address into a complete monitoring setup in about 15 minutes. It has 7 steps: Setup, What you offer, Topics, Prompts, Platforms, Competitors, and Review & launch. A preview pane on the side shows your weekly cadence and how many prompts you are tracking as you go, and you can leave anytime with "Save draft & exit".

## Step 1: Setup

Headline: "See how AI talks about your brand."

Enter your business name, website address, and location. Location matters: it tailors every generated question to your market, so a plumber in Leeds gets different prompts than one in Austin.

Two optional fields are worth your attention:

- **Mention aliases**: other names AI might call you (old names, abbreviations, common misspellings). Anything listed here counts as your brand when answers are analyzed.
- **Track this subfolder only**: limits monitoring to pages under one path, useful for a location page on a larger website.

When you continue, Visibility Suite reads your website and researches your business on the web to fill in the next step.

## Step 2: What you offer

Headline: "Refine the brand AI sees."

The wizard shows what its research found: what your business does, your industry, market and location, language, products and services, brand tags, themes, and AI-detected audience segments. Everything is editable, and a clearer profile means sharper prompts and more accurate scoring. Use "Regenerate" to re-extract from your site if something looks off. A free-text field at the bottom captures anything else AI should know, like your positioning or markets you avoid.

## Step 3: Topics

Headline: "Choose the conversations that matter."

The wizard suggests market-level topic clusters, like "hotels near the convention center" or "wedding catering", each chosen because an AI answer to it would name several businesses. Keep the ones you want to be known for, and add custom topics of your own. Each kept topic gets its own set of questions in the next step.

## Step 4: Prompts

Headline: "These are the questions we'll ask."

For every topic, the wizard generates questions written the way real customers ask: anchored to your market, free of your brand name, with buying intent ("best family dentist in Plano", not "tell me about Plano Dental Group"). Each prompt carries an intent label and an estimated monthly volume. You can edit wording, regenerate a batch, or type your own questions. Everything remains editable after launch.

## Step 5: Platforms

Headline: "Which AI engines should we cover?"

Pick the AI assistants to monitor: ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot, Grok, Google AI Overview, and Google AI Mode. Choose the ones your customers actually use. You can change this later in Settings.

## Step 6: Competitors

Headline: "Benchmark against your rivals."

Use "Find competitors with AI" to search the web for real, currently operating direct competitors in your market and price tier, each suggested with a website and a one-line reason. Keep the suggestions that matter, or add competitors manually. This step is optional: you can add competitors anytime after launch.

## Step 7: Review & launch

Headline: "Everything look right?"

The review screen shows the full setup and flags anything missing before you launch: a business name and website, at least one prompt, at least one AI platform. Choose "Confirm & start monitoring" to go live, or "Save as draft" to launch later.

After launch, monitoring runs weekly by default. Each run asks every active prompt on every platform you picked and saves the full answers. Change the schedule or trigger a manual run from Settings.
