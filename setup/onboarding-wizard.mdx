title: "The onboarding wizard"
description: "A step-by-step tour of the 6-step setup wizard, what AI fills in at each step, and what happens when you launch."
---

The onboarding wizard turns your website address into a complete monitoring setup in about 5 minutes. It has 6 steps: Setup, Business profile, Topics & questions, Platforms, Competitors, and Review & launch. A preview pane on the side shows how many topics and questions you are tracking as you go, and you can leave anytime with "Save draft & exit".

## Step 1: Setup

Headline: "Create your project."

Enter your business name, website address, and location. Location matters: it tailors every generated question to your market, so a plumber in Leeds gets different prompts than one in Austin. A deep link is fine; you do not need to find the homepage first.

Two optional fields are worth your attention:

- **Brand names**: other names AI might call you (old names, abbreviations, common misspellings). Anything listed here counts as your brand when answers are analyzed.
- **Tracking scope**: controls how much of the site we monitor.
  - A URL with a path, such as `https://www.example.com/en-us/hotels/acme`, defaults to **Subfolder only**. We monitor that section and everything under it.
  - A root URL, such as `https://example.com/`, `https://www.example.com/`, or a subdomain such as `https://app.example.com/`, tracks the **Full domain**.
  - You can switch a deep URL to **Full domain**, but the wizard warns that every page on the host will be included, not just the subfolder.
  - `www` and non-`www` versions of the same path are treated the same. A different path is a different scope.

<Note>
  If you are setting up one location on a shared brand site, keep the subfolder default. It prevents the location from inheriting citations and mentions from the rest of the domain.
</Note>

When you continue, Visibility Suite reads your website and researches your business on the web to fill in the next step.

## Step 2: Business profile

Headline: "Business profile."

The wizard shows what its research found, grouped into three sections:

- **Overview**: description, industry, market and location, language.
- **Offerings & positioning**: products and services, brand tags, themes, and optional business context.
- **Audiences**: AI-detected audience segments in priority order.

Everything is editable. Use **Regenerate** in the Audiences section to re-extract from your site if something looks off. A clearer profile means better topics, better questions, and more accurate scoring.

## Step 3: Topics & questions

Headline: "Choose what to monitor."

The AI suggests up to **10 market-level topics** for your business and automatically writes about **five questions** under each one. Topics group the conversations; questions are the exact prompts we send to the AI engines.

- Keep, edit, or remove any topic or question.
- Add your own question inside a topic, or add a topic the AI missed.
- Use **Write with AI** / **Regenerate with AI** inside a topic to refresh just that topic's questions.
- **Regenerate topics** replaces the AI-suggested list while keeping topics you added yourself.
- Going Back and forward through the wizard does not regenerate anything. The buttons only call the AI when you ask them to.

Questions are written at the market level and do not include your brand name. That is deliberate: a question like "best Italian restaurant in Tempe" forces AI to choose among businesses, while "is Tony's Italian good?" only tests one business and teaches you nothing about your competitive position.

<Note>
  The AI targets 10 topics and 5 questions per topic. If the model returns fewer, the backend asks for the remainder before showing the step. You can always add or generate more yourself.
</Note>

## Step 4: Platforms

Headline: "Choose AI engines."

Pick the AI assistants to monitor: ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot, Grok, Google AI Overview, and Google AI Mode. Choose the ones your customers actually use. You can change this later in Settings.

## Step 5: Competitors

Headline: "Add competitors."

Use "Find competitors with AI" to search the web for real, currently operating direct competitors in your market and price tier, each suggested with a website and a one-line reason. Keep the suggestions that matter, or add competitors manually. This step is optional: you can add competitors anytime after launch.

## Step 6: Review & launch

Headline: "Review everything."

The review screen shows the full setup and flags anything missing before you launch: a business name and website, at least one question, and at least one AI platform. Choose "Confirm & start monitoring" to go live, or "Save as draft" to launch later. Every section has an **Edit** link back to the relevant step.

After launch, monitoring runs weekly by default. Each run asks every active question on every platform you picked and saves the full answers. Change the schedule or trigger a manual run from Settings.
