Visibility Suite shows you what AI assistants say about your business, and gives you a clear plan to get recommended more often.

When someone asks ChatGPT, Gemini, or Perplexity "what's the best dentist in Austin?", the answer decides who gets the call. Visibility Suite asks those questions for you every week, across every major AI platform, and tracks whether your business is mentioned, cited as a source, and recommended, alongside the competitors AI picks instead of you.

## What you can do with it

<CardGroup cols={2}>

## Which AI platforms are covered

Monitoring runs across seven AI surfaces: ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot, Grok, Google AI Overview, and Google AI Mode. Google search results are tracked alongside AI answers, so you can see traditional rankings and AI visibility in one place.

## Who it is for

Visibility Suite is built for local and multi-location businesses: restaurants, hotels, dental and medical practices, home services, salons, law firms, and 40 more industries. Setup tailors itself to your industry, and you do not need any technical background to use it.
